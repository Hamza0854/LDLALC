Our Heritage

Leeton and District Local Aboriginal Land Council — History

Leeton is located in the country of the Wiradjuri nation, and many members of our community are Wiradjuri people. Some of our people came originally from Narrandera and Darlington Point.

Our community also embraces many people descended from other language groups: Yota Yota people from Victoria came here when Cummeragunja station broke up in the 1930s. Ngarigo people from the southern highlands also lived in our area and Wemba Wemba people have been members since the 1970s.

Our Land Council has been the focal point for programs, services and activities in the Leeton district since its incorporation in 1984.

"Our community is diverse and strong — united by our deep connection to the Wiradjuri country and our shared commitment to cultural preservation and community wellbeing."

— L&DLALC Community Statement

Land Boundaries

Leeton and District Local Aboriginal Land Council — Boundaries

The area for which LDLALC has responsibility totals about 3,500 km². At its widest point, the distance from east to west is about 50 km and north to south, 105 km. The corresponding local government authority is Leeton Shire Council.

Structure & Rules

Governance

The Primary Rules under which L&DLALC Operates. The legislation under which LDLALC is constituted, and the objectives which the Land Council is obliged to pursue, is stated in Section 1.1 of the Aboriginal Land Rights Act.

Aboriginal Land Rights Act

LDLALC operates under the Aboriginal Land Rights Act 1983 (NSW). This legislation defines the objects, objectives and obligations of all Local Aboriginal Land Councils across NSW.

Primary Rules

The Primary Rules set out procedures for meetings, elections, financial management, and decision-making within the Land Council as required under the ALRA.

Annual General Meetings

L&DLALC holds Annual General Meetings open to all members — essential for elections, financial reporting and community decision-making on land matters.

Board of Directors

A democratically elected Board of Directors governs the Land Council and is responsible for overall management, strategic direction and community accountability.

Financial Accountability

All financial accounts are independently audited annually. Reports are presented to members at the AGM in strict accordance with ALRA requirements.

Community Consultation

The Land Council is committed to regular community consultation to ensure all decisions reflect the needs and aspirations of Aboriginal people in the Leeton area.

In the words of the Act, "the object of Local Aboriginal Land Councils is to improve, protect and foster the best interests of all Aboriginal persons within their area." L&DLALC takes this responsibility seriously in every decision made on behalf of our community.

Join Us

Membership

All applications for membership must be accompanied by a certified copy of the applicant's full birth certificate and a 'Confirmation of Aboriginality' certificate from a recognised Aboriginal Organisation.

Eligibility Requirements

Must be of Aboriginal descent and identify as an Aboriginal person
Must be accepted by the Aboriginal community in the LDLALC area
Must be 18 years of age or older at the time of application
Provide certified copy of full birth certificate
Provide 'Confirmation of Aboriginality' from a recognised incorporated Aboriginal Organisation
If not a direct descendant of an L&DLALC member, additional documentation may be required

How to Apply

1
Gather Your Documents

Obtain a certified copy of your full birth certificate and a Confirmation of Aboriginality from a recognised Aboriginal organisation.

2
Complete Application Form

Collect an official membership application form from the LDLALC office at Shop 1/5 Belah Street, Leeton NSW 2705.

3
Submit Your Application

Lodge your completed application along with all required documents in person or by post to our Leeton office.

4
Await Confirmation

The Board will review your application. Approved members will receive a membership certificate.

Ready to Become a Member?

Contact our office today to begin your membership application and become part of the L&DLALC community.
